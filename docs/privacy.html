WraithDesk Privacy Policy

Insane Zombie Studio · Contact: insnzmb@gmail.com

Last updated: November 13, 2026.

WraithDesk lets a paired Meta Quest headset view and control a Windows PC on the local network. The current application has no Insane Zombie Studio account service, advertising SDK, analytics service, cloud relay or automatic desktop recording.

Information used

  • Desktop pictures/video and Windows system playback audio travel from your PC to a trusted headset while connected. Screens may contain sensitive information. Audio sharing is controlled in Settings.
  • Optional headset microphone audio is captured only after you enable forwarding and grant permission, and after the paired PC acknowledges the microphone route. It starts off each launch, is buffered briefly in memory, and stops on focus loss, pause, revocation or disconnect. WraithDesk does not save an audio recording. Windows apps you choose may record or transmit it under their own settings and policies. Setup uses a local USB audio server and a separately configured, unchanged Microsoft-signed open-source USB transport; Windows may request administrator approval. The server listens on the PC's loopback interface.
  • Sound-reactive screen lighting analyzes playback audio locally. A Windows helper associates active audio sessions with monitors and sends monitor identifiers and availability to the headset. It does not send window titles, process names or an additional copy of the audio to Insane Zombie Studio. Shared browser audio processes can make monitor association ambiguous.
  • Pointer movements, clicks, typed keys and configured shortcuts travel from the headset to the PC to operate Windows. WraithDesk does not intentionally record a typing history.
  • When you enable gamepad forwarding, the selected regular controller's buttons, sticks and triggers travel from the headset to the paired PC as virtual gamepad input. Forwarding starts off each launch and stops on pause, focus loss, revocation or disconnect. WraithDesk does not record a gamepad input history. No hand or body poses are included in these reports.
  • PC discovery uses local network addresses and a computer name. Discovery does not grant access.
  • Pairing creates a per-PC TLS identity and per-headset access token. The PC stores the token hash and a device label, identifier and pairing time. The headset stores its token and trusted PC certificate in its private app storage.
  • Saved workspace data includes screen positions, sizes, orientations, settings, shortcuts and bindings.
  • Hand joint and controller poses are used locally to aim and manipulate controls. When available, inferred arm joint poses are used locally to keep wrist-docked screens aligned. WraithDesk does not store or send hand or body tracking data, or raw passthrough camera images, to the PC.
  • If you choose room attachment and grant Spatial Data permission, the headset uses Meta's room surfaces and anchor identifiers. Saved layout may contain anchor identifiers and offsets. WraithDesk does not upload room scans to an Insane Zombie Studio service.
  • Bounded local diagnostic logs record service startup, connection errors and display/capture metadata. They are not automatically sent to us. Do not send logs or screenshots containing private content without reviewing them.

Sharing and security

Desktop media, keyboard and pointer input use authenticated, certificate-pinned TLS. Optional gamepad forwarding negotiates a fresh session through the same trusted TLS connection, then uses encrypted and authenticated UDP when available, with a separate TLS input fallback. Compare the identity code shown on the PC and headset before approving the first connection. A temporary code authorizes pairing. Use a network you trust. Pause closes active access; revoking a headset removes its PC authorization. There is no automatic plaintext fallback.

Microsoft, Meta and any store platform may process platform data under their own policies. This notice describes WraithDesk's application behavior and does not replace those policies.

Retention and controls

PC app data is in %LOCALAPPDATA%\MRSpace. Headset data is in the application's private storage. Revoke devices in the PC manager and forget the PC on the headset. Uninstalling the PC companion preserves settings by default; its optional remove-data choice deletes PC pairings, settings and logs. Android uninstall normally removes the headset app's local data. Room scans managed by Meta are controlled in headset settings. No cloud copy is retained by the current WraithDesk application.

If you email support, Insane Zombie Studio receives the information you choose to send. Avoid attaching passwords, full desktop captures or private documents. Contact insnzmb@gmail.com for privacy questions, requests to correct or delete support correspondence, or security vulnerability reports. Insane Zombie Studio deletes its support correspondence after the issue is resolved. Email providers may retain information under their own policies.

Optional app pull-out reads running app names and window titles locally and shows them only to a trusted paired headset. It does not send them to Insane Zombie Studio or save a window-title history. The PC temporarily saves original window positions and process/window identifiers so it can return apps after a disconnect or interrupted session; that recovery metadata is deleted when the apps are returned.
